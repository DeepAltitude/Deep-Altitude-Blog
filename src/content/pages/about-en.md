I write primarily to understand things myself. A thought often seems clear in my head. Writing it down shows me what matters, what is only an assumption, and where I still contradict myself.

DeepAltitude is a public notebook. It holds things I experience, learn and try to understand. Some entries are short and unfinished. Others are longer attempts to put my thoughts in order.

**Sportas** is about physical practice and adventure. **Kalbos** is about languages and learning how to learn. **Protas** is about thinking, decisions, relationships and life.

These are notes from experience and my current understanding. There is room to change my mind. I want to return to them later and ask: do I still think this? What has experience shown me?

The notebook is public. If something here is useful to you too, good.
